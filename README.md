# East Africa Digital Governance Index (2026)
Can East African governments deliver digital public services at scale — and what's actually holding them back?
This project analyses structural barriers to e-government adoption across 7 EAC countries, testing the hypothesis that institutional capacity, not connectivity infrastructure, is the primary binding constraint to digital public service delivery.

The Question
Development finance institutions and policymakers frequently invest in broadband and mobile infrastructure to accelerate digital government. But is connectivity actually the bottleneck? This analysis builds a comparative framework to identify whether countries are infrastructure-constrained, capacity-constrained, or both — and what that means for where investment and policy attention should go next.
